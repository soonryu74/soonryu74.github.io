## Title
The Opening (입구)

## Theme
Human Relationships

## Logline
A daughter takes her estranged father back into the cave he found twenty years ago to restore his erased name, and learns that the child who fell into that opening was her.

## Synopsis
Jeju, 2005. During utility-pole work, the ground gives way beneath a labourer, Dong-su. A yellow work glove falls into the dark, and he tells no one what happened.

Years later his daughter Hae-in, now a cave surveyor, is asked in public whether the worker who found the cave is her father. With him standing at the back of the room, she answers, "I don't know him."

On the eve of the cave's permanent closure she brings him inside, determined to put his name in the record. Deep in the cave they find the glove. He tells her not to. It did not fall from his hand. It fell from hers: she was seven, playing in his gloves, when the ground opened and he caught her by the wrist. She did not speak for a year, and he carried the village's blame so it would never reach her.

In total darkness on the underground lake, she speaks first.

## Director's note
The film is about two people who could not say one sentence to each other. Hands carry the story: a hand that caught a child, a hand that stops before touching a cave wall, and two hands found resting together when the light comes back on.
