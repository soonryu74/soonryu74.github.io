# v8 추가 시트 프롬프트 (Gemini 이미지, 16:9)

기존 시트 8장(`../v7/12_character_sheet_prompts_v7.md`로 만든 것)은 그대로 쓴다. v8에서 새로 필요한 것은 아래 2장이다.

## 해인 7세 (CH_H07) — 해인(현재) 시트 첨부
```
Using the attached reference, create a character reference sheet of the same person as a 7-year-old child, so that she clearly looks like the same girl twenty years younger: same eye shape, same face outline. A small Korean girl with a short black bob haircut, wearing a yellow raincoat and small yellow rain boots. Show her four times in a row: (1) full-body front view, plain; (2) full-body front view wearing her father's oversized white hard hat that sits too low on her head and oversized bright yellow rubber-coated work gloves with beige knit cuffs that are far too big for her hands, holding a cloth-wrapped lunch bundle; (3) close-up of her face smiling; (4) close-up of her face wide-eyed and silent. Plain light-grey background, soft even lighting, photorealistic, 16:9. No text, no logos.
```

## 도시락 보자기 (PR_LUNCH)
```
Prop reference sheet, photorealistic, 16:9, plain light-grey background, soft even lighting. A Korean lunch bundle: a stacked stainless-steel lunch tin wrapped and knotted in a faded blue-and-white checked cloth. Shown twice: front view and three-quarter view. No text, no logos.
```

## 주의
- 아이 시트는 얼굴이 현재 해인과 이어지는지가 전부다. 닮지 않으면 다시 뽑는다.
- 실존 아역 배우를 닮게 나오면 버린다.
