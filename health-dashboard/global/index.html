    About this prototype

    What it is — and is not

    Health Equity Radar was first built for the Republic of Korea (health-profile.kr), using official sub-national statistics to show which local health problems to review first and why. This Global prototype applies the same idea between countries: each indicator is compared with countries in the same World Bank income group, signals are explained with the numbers behind them, and possible action areas are listed from a transparent rule file.

    • Exploratory. Signals and the score are for screening and prioritization, not clinical or causal inference, and they do not predict disease or policy effects.
    • Latest available, not current. Every value carries its own observation year; years differ by indicator and country.
    • Open data only. World Bank World Development Indicators; no restricted microdata.
    • Transparent rules. Directions, thresholds and weights are written in the code and on the methodology page.

    Data: World Bank, World Development Indicators (WDI), extract dated 2026-10-01 — licensed CC BY 4.0 per the WDI series metadata. Underlying sources include UN Population Division, WHO, UNICEF, UN IGME, ILO, UNESCO UIS, the WHO/UNICEF JMP and the World Bank; indicator-level sources are shown with each indicator and on the methodology page.
