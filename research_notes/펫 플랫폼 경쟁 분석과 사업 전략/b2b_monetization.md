# B2B Monetization Benchmarks for 펫안심365 Partner Plans (Korea first, global second; as of 2024–2026)

Scope note: research was done on 2026-09-25. Years are labeled on each finding. Items marked **[unverified]** come from secondary or vendor-marketing sources, or could not be confirmed against a primary source. Where search returned nothing reliable, the item is listed under Gaps.

---

## 1. What do Korean local pet businesses currently pay for customer acquisition and clinic software?

### Takeaway
A Korean pet shop or clinic that relies on free listings spends almost nothing. Naver Smart Place, Naver 예약 and 당근 비즈프로필 are all free to set up. Paid acquisition runs on CPC: Naver Place ads start at 50 KRW per click and 당근 at 100 KRW, with local vet search budgets commonly quoted at about 300,000–500,000 KRW a month. Clinic EMR/CRM software costs about 0–44,000 KRW per vet per month, plus about 22,000 KRW for each add-on. So a 59,000 KRW Pro plan costs more than a basic EMR seat but much less than a typical monthly ad budget.

### Cited Findings
**Naver (Smart Place / Place ads / Power Link / 예약)**
- Place ads cost nothing to register or show. They are CPC: you pay only for clicks. Keyword bids run from **50 KRW minimum to 5,000 KRW maximum**, and a Smart Place registration is required first (2025–26 guide) — [Toss Place 사장님 스토리](https://tossplace.com/story/smartplace-ads); [마케팅 인사이드](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/9129)
- 2026 guide (updated 2026-07-11): Place ads start at 50 KRW/click and Power Link search ads at 70 KRW/click. Clicks in competitive areas were observed at **800–900 KRW**. The recommended ad budget is **5–10% of target monthly revenue** (for example, about 1.5M KRW on a 30M KRW revenue goal) **[vendor blog]** — [플레이스비서 (euncoach)](https://ai.euncoach.com/guide/naver-place-ad-cost)
- For vet clinics, a Naver search budget of **300,000–500,000 KRW a month** is said to be enough for local keyword exposure. The same source suggests a marketing budget of 5–10% of monthly revenue, rising to 10–15% for new clinics **[agency marketing content; unverified]** — [더상승마케팅 동물병원 마케팅](https://increasemarketing.co.kr/animal-hospital-marketing/)
- Naver 예약 has **no monthly fee**. Businesses that take bookings and collect payment on-site pay nothing. If Naver Pay is connected for prepayment, an order-management fee applies: **1.98% for micro businesses (≤300M KRW annual sales), 2.585% / 2.75% / 3.025% for SME tiers, and 3.63% general, VAT included** (fee table as of 2025). The June 2025 abolition of the 2% inflow fee applied to Smart Store only, not to 예약 — [Omago 네이버 예약 수수료](https://www.omago.ai/ko/blog/naver-booking-fees-truth-korea); [나무위키 네이버 예약](https://namu.wiki/w/%EB%84%A4%EC%9D%B4%EB%B2%84%20%EC%98%88%EC%95%BD)

**당근 (Daangn) 비즈프로필 / ads**
- Registering a 비즈프로필 is free. In expert mode the minimum daily budget is 10,000 KRW and the minimum CPC is 100 KRW. Simple mode can start at 5,000 KRW a day. Average CPC in Seoul and Gyeonggi is **300–600 KRW** **[agency / blog sources, 2025–26]** — [아이보스](https://www.i-boss.co.kr/ab-2987-367796); [Metric Lab 2026](https://lab.studioplanc.com/daangn-ads-cost-2026/); [당근비즈니스 교육](https://business.daangn.com/edu/vod/fc48340c-4ced-4253-9ab2-590f2ed625d2/%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC-%EA%B4%91%EA%B3%A0-%EC%98%88%EC%82%B0-%EC%9D%BC%EC%A0%95)

**Kakao 채널 messaging**
- 친구톡 ended on 2025-12-31. From 2026-01-01, all 친구톡 sends switch to 브랜드메시지. Agency list prices are about **23–25 KRW per message to channel friends** and **33–35 KRW to non-friends who opted in** (VAT excluded) — [Omago 카카오 단가](https://www.omago.ai/ko/blog/kakaotalk-message-pricing-logic); [FlareLane](https://flarelane.com/ko/blog/kakao-brandmessage/)
- Kakao's own one-year review (Aug 2026) says small partners earned **11.1 KRW of sales per 1 KRW of send cost**, with sales +90.2%, repurchase +47.3% and visitors +22.6%. This is a company-reported figure — [아시아경제 2026-08-25](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026082511302320102); [아주경제](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20260825111349657)

**Platform ad/fee burden on Korean small businesses (context)**
- 2024 중기중앙회 survey (n=1,103, fieldwork Apr–Jun 2024). Average monthly platform ad spend was **1,207,263 KRW for online malls, 1,079,300 KRW for accommodation apps** (822,200 KRW exposure ads plus 257,100 KRW coupon ads) and **107,780 KRW for delivery apps** — [아시아경제 2024-07-03](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2024070316214179981)
- 2026 중기중앙회 survey (n=1,250, fieldwork Jun 18–Jul 3 2026, released 2026-09-15). Delivery-app merchants pay **26.2% of monthly revenue** in fees and ads (+5.2 percentage points year over year). The average across all platforms is 21.7%, and accommodation apps are 18.3%. Over 51% of merchants want a total fee cap — [아시아경제 2026-09-15](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026091515244632297)

**Clinic EMR / CRM / reminder SaaS in Korea**
- **플러스벳 (벳칭)** publishes per-vet monthly prices (VAT excluded):
  - Basic **0 KRW**: recall, appointments, SOAP charting, payment
  - Standard **22,000 KRW**: adds automated visit-purpose SMS, digital intake forms and 1:1 messaging
  - Pro **44,000 KRW**: adds treatment/prescription orders and group messaging
  - Add-ons at **22,000 KRW/month each**: queue system, unattended reception, **Naver 예약 integration**; AI, SMS and calls are usage-based — [플러스벳 요금제](https://vetching.cc/pricing)
- **인투벳 (인투씨엔에스)** has been used in about 2,000 animal hospitals since 2008. It launched "인투벳 클라우드" in Feb 2026 in two pricing models (차트형 / 카피형), but no prices are published. An older tablet add-on (인투패드) had a monthly management fee of 10,000 KRW + VAT — [인투씨엔에스 공지](https://www.intocns.com/bbs/notice_view.php?idx=31); [네이트뉴스 2026-02-23](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260223n29679)
- Korean vet EMR options are few: mainly 이프렌즈, 인투벳 and 우리엔, with newer entrants such as 플러스벳. 인투벳 and 우리엔 offer guardian apps that show vaccination and test records linked to the chart **[practitioner social post]** — [Threads @jiman_yoon](https://www.threads.com/@jiman_yoon/post/DVWHtVxEswS/)

**Market size of addressable partners**
- 2024 pet-business premises (sale, production, import, boarding, transport, funeral, grooming, etc.) totaled **23,565**, up 14.5% from 20,575 in 2023, with 29,305 workers. Animal hospitals are not in this count; they are licensed under 수의사법 — [농림축산식품부 보도자료](https://www.mafra.go.kr/bbs/home/792/574273/artclView.do)
- The 2022 national survey found animal hospitals were the most-used pet service, at 71.8% — [same MAFRA release](https://www.mafra.go.kr/bbs/home/792/574273/artclView.do)

### Inferences
- A pet shop's **realistic monthly acquisition spend** likely falls between about 0 KRW (free Smart Place, 비즈프로필 and Kakao channel only) and a few hundred thousand KRW for CPC ads. Vet clinics are at the upper end, and the 300–500K KRW figure fits. At **59,000 KRW**, Pro equals roughly 60–120 Naver Place clicks at 500–1,000 KRW each. It has to be sold on measurable inquiries or calls, not on the badge alone.
- Korea anchors free booking, free profiles and free basic EMR, which makes a paid "hours/services management" feature hard to sell. It exists free on Naver. The paid value has to come from **trust verification plus leads that Naver does not deliver**, such as safety-intent searches, emergency and night-care routing, and pet-hotel safety checks.
- The 22,000 KRW price for a "Naver 예약 integration" add-on in vet EMR suggests clinics will pay about 20,000 KRW a month for a single integration. That is a reasonable price point for a narrow Pro-lite or add-on tier.

### Gaps
- No primary survey found on monthly marketing spend by category for **grooming, pet hotels, pet cafés, funeral or trainers**. 소상공인시장진흥공단 did not surface pet-specific data.
- **우리엔 and 이프렌즈 prices** are not published. 인투벳 Cloud prices are not published.
- No verified figure for the total number of animal hospitals in 2024–2025 (commonly cited as about 5,000; **unverified**, not sourced here).
- No verified data on Instagram/Meta CPC or CPM for Korean local pet businesses.

## 2. Korean vertical-platform analogs with local-business subscriptions/ads, and regulatory issues

### Takeaway
The Korean analogs that make money do so through **pay-for-visibility** (CPV/CPC/flat-fee placement) or **per-lead fees**, not through pure SaaS subscriptions. Almost every one has run into regulators or a merchant backlash: 강남언니 over patient inducement and minimum ad deposits, 야놀자/여기어때 over coupon-ad bundles, and 배민 when it dropped its flat-fee ad. A trust-positioned pet platform should favor transparent flat subscriptions and clear ad labels.

### Cited Findings
**강남언니 (Healing Paper)**
- Ads are billed **CPV (cost per view)** when a logged-in user opens a hospital, doctor or event page. Billing does not depend on consultations or visits. Unit price is set dynamically by "AI" from ad duration, past performance, rating and category (2025 partner doc) — [강남언니 CPV 소개서](https://docs.channel.io/unni/ko/articles/CPV%EA%B3%BC%EA%B8%88-%EB%B0%A9%EC%8B%9D-%EC%86%8C%EA%B0%9C%EC%84%9C--e045a4e3); [partner-with-gangnamunni](https://partner-with-gangnamunni.oopy.io/33f016de-4c73-43cd-8af2-5fd3d1e2cc47)
- Clinics reportedly had to deposit **at least 2M KRW in ad credit** or their hospital name would not appear in the app at all, and plastic surgeons complained. The article date was not confirmed because the page returned a 503 — [메디칼타임즈](https://www.medicaltimes.com/Main/News/NewsView.html?ID=1157118) (headline and snippet only)
- Revenue was **75.2B KRW in 2025, up 41.9% from 53.0B KRW in 2024** — [아웃스탠딩 via X](https://x.com/outstandcrew/status/2054038509158347251); [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/healingpaper/financials). It had about 1,800 partner hospitals as of July 2022 **[older figure]** — [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/healingpaper). It first turned an operating profit around 2023 — [딜사이트](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/124815)
- **Regulatory:** the CEO was convicted under **의료법 제27조** (patient introduction or inducement for profit) for selling procedure coupons and taking a cut of their value. The sentence was 8 months, suspended for 2 years, and the appeal court upheld it — [법률신문](http://www.lawtimes.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=189061); [메디게이트](https://medigatenews.com/news/2575038076). A bill letting such platforms show procedure prices passed the 복지위 committee (date not confirmed) — [메디칼타임즈](https://www.medicaltimes.com/Users/News/NewsView.html?ID=1152743)
- July 2026: 공정위 sanctioned **three Gangnam plastic-surgery clinics** for undisclosed paid reviews ("윤곽 수술 만족해요" 뒷광고) on beauty-medical platforms. The fetched text had encoding problems, so the exact respondents and penalty should be checked — [네이트뉴스 2026-07-12](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260712n06493)

**똑닥 (Vibros)**
- Moved to a **consumer-paid** membership of 1,000 KRW a month or 10,000 KRW a year on 2023-09-05, after about 7 years of losses. This drew criticism over "medical access by payment." No hospital-side subscription price was found — [세계일보 2023-12-21](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20231221503965); [베이비뉴스](https://www.ibabynews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=112206); [경향신문](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202312062212005)

**모두닥 / 굿닥**
- 모두닥 lets hospitals upload profiles, photos, hours and "events" through a partner admin. No ad prices were found — [모두닥 파트너](https://www.partner.modoodoc.com/)

**배달의민족 울트라콜 (flat-fee history)**
- 울트라콜 cost **88,000 KRW per "flag" per month** for exposure in a chosen area, with no commission. It was criticized for causing flag-bidding wars and was **phased out region by region from April 2025**, leaving the commission-based 오픈리스트. Franchisee groups said they would report 배민 to 공정위 — [한국경제 2025-01-31](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025013162291); [서울경제](https://www.sedaily.com/NewsView/2GP1KUKKCF); [네이트 2025-03-12](https://news.nate.com/view/20250312n35721)

**야놀자 / 여기어때 (accommodation "사장님 광고")**
- 공정위 imposed corrective orders and **15.4B KRW in surcharges** (야놀자 5.4B, 여기어때 10.0B) for abuse of dominant position. Since 2017 they had sold "ad plus coupon" bundles and let unused coupons expire unilaterally: 야놀자 coupons were worth 10–25% of ad spend, 여기어때 up to 29%, and expired coupons totaled about 1.2B and 35.9B KRW respectively — [법률신문](https://www.lawtimes.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=210486); [시사저널](https://www.sisajournal.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=342683)
- Prosecutors then **raided both companies on 2026-03-10** over the same "ad and coupon 갑질" allegations — [네이트뉴스 2026-03-10](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260310n24427); [뉴스1](https://www.news1.kr/industry/hotel-tourism/6039097)

**숨고 (lead pricing)**
- Pros pay per **quote sent**, not per transaction, using prepaid "숨고캐시". Cost depends on category, region and competition. About **900 cash per quote** is often cited **[unverified; blog]**. Prices were re-set by service and region on **2025-08-20**, and 숨고페이 charges a 3.5% payment fee — [숨고 도움말](https://help.soomgo.com/hc/ko/articles/360053164232); [퇴근후30분](https://afterwork30.com/reviews/soomgo-pro-cost-structure); [닷클](https://dotcle.kr/articles/?bmode=view&idx=170069454)

**Pet-vertical Korean analogs**
- These operate in Korea: 펫닥, 펫프라이스 (vet price quotes), 펫트라슈 (vet fee lookup and AI reviews), 닥터펫 ("동물병원 성장 플랫폼" connecting clinics and companies through campaigns) and 비마이펫 (clinic website and marketing agency, BFV). **None publish partner pricing** — [펫닥](https://www.petdoc.co.kr/); [펫트라슈](https://www.petraschu.com/); [닥터펫](https://www.petsneeds.co.kr/); [비마이펫 BFV](https://vet.bemypet.kr/)

### Inferences
- **Flat fees are popular with merchants but hard for platforms to sustain.** 울트라콜 at 88,000 KRW/month shows Korean small merchants will pay a known flat fee for exposure. A 59,000 KRW Pro plan sits below that anchor. Flat fees tied to *ranking* caused arms races, so 펫안심365 should keep ranking **separate** from paid tiers and sell verification and tools rather than position.
- **Avoid take-rate or per-booking commissions for vet clinics.** The 강남언니 conviction under 의료법 §27 is a human-medicine precedent. 수의사법 시행령 treats external-platform discounts as patient inducement (see Q4). Together they make commissions on vet treatment risky. A flat SaaS subscription is safer.
- **Coupons in the Growth tier must not be "ad plus coupon" bundles** in which the platform gains from unused coupons (야놀자/여기어때). Coupons should be merchant-funded and merchant-owned, and unused value should never flow to the platform.
- The 강남언니 "2M KRW minimum deposit to be listed" backlash shows why the **Basic free public-data profile should stay permanently visible**, so the platform does not look like it is charging to exist.

### Gaps
- No public price lists found for 강남언니 CPV unit rates, 여기어때/야놀자 사장님 ad products, 모두닥/굿닥 hospital ads, 크몽 seller fees (not searched in depth), or 당근 비즈니스 subscription products.
- No public **free-to-paid conversion rates** for any Korean local-business platform (Smart Place, 당근, 강남언니, 숨고).

## 3. Global analogs (Yelp, PetDesk/Vetstoria/Weave, Rover/Wag, Google Business Profile) and free→paid conversion

### Takeaway
Global clinic-communication SaaS costs **US$199–$497+ a month** (about 270K–680K KRW), so a 149,000 KRW Growth plan is cheap by US standards. Yelp's paid **Verified License badge at US$1/day** (about US$30/month) is the closest analog to a paid "verified badge" and suggests a verification-only add-on should cost roughly 30,000–45,000 KRW. Self-serve freemium typically converts **2–5%** of free users, and sales-assisted freemium converts 5–7%.

### Cited Findings
**Yelp**
- **Verified License** launched in 2019 as a paid product at **US$1 per day** for qualified service providers such as contractors, plumbers and movers. Yelp manually verifies a valid state trade license. Businesses that bought it saw an **average +10% in calls, clicks and map views** within 30 days (company-reported). It shows as a blue shield with a check mark — [Yelp IR 2019](https://www.yelp-ir.com/news/press-releases/news-release-details/2019/Yelp-Announces-Verified-Licenses-For-Service-Providers/default.aspx); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/yelp-adds-verified-licenses-for-local-providers-to-lineup-of-paid-products-313473); [Yelp support](https://biz.yelp.com/support-center/Advertising_on_Yelp/Other_Paid_Products/What-is-the-Verified-License-badge/en-US)
- BrightLocal questioned whether Yelp is "monetizing consumer trust" with a paid verified badge — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/blog/is-yelp-monetizing-consumer-trust-with-its-new-verified-badge/)
- Yelp ads are CPC at about **US$2–10+ per click**. SMBs typically budget US$300–1,000 a month, and cost per lead is often US$10+ **[agency sources, 2025–26]** — [WebFX 2026](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ppc/yelp-advertising-costs/); [J&S Digital](https://www.jandsdigital.com/cost-of-yelp-ads-real-2025)

**Vet clinic SaaS**
- **PetDesk**: the app plus email appointment requests starts at **US$389/month**. Vetstoria direct booking is +US$200/month as an add-on or **US$350/month** standalone. Another listing gives a US$249/month starting price (sources conflict; 2026 listings) — [VetSoftwareHub PetDesk](https://www.vetsoftwarehub.com/product/petdesk/pricing); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/petdesk/pricing/)
- **Vetstoria** (international; operates as PetDesk in the US): Small Team **US$230**, Medium **US$323** and Large **US$462 per month** — [Vetstoria pricing](https://www.vetstoria.com/pricing/); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/vetstoria/pricing)
- **Weave**: Base **US$199/month** (reminders, two-way texting, scheduling, phones). Coordinate and Attract bundles are +US$149 each, so Base + Coordinate + Attract is **US$497/month** (2026 third-party summaries) — [Emitrr](https://emitrr.com/blog/weave-pricing/); [Weave pricing](https://www.getweave.com/pricing/)

**Pet-care marketplaces (take rates)**
- **Rover** takes **20%** from sitters as standard, 25% for RoverGO and California. It also charges owners an **11% booking fee capped at US$50**. It is piloting tiered fees that start at **30% for new clients** **[third-party sitter tools, 2026]**. **Wag** takes about **40%** — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/finance/learn/rover-vs-wag); [Scritches](https://scritches.io/blog/rover-tiered-fees); [sidehustles.com](https://sidehustles.com/rover-app-review/)

**Google Business Profile**
- Not researched in this pass. GBP verification is generally free, but that is **not cited here**; see Gaps.

**Free→paid conversion benchmarks**
- Self-serve freemium typically converts **3–5%**, with top performers at 6–8%. Sales-assisted freemium averages **5–7%**, with top performers at 10–15% — [First Page Sage 2026 freemium report](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-freemium-conversion-rates/) (consultancy dataset; methodology not audited)
- A typical self-serve freemium cohort converts **2–5%**, measured over 3–6 months — [Fiscallion](https://www.fiscallion.io/blog/freemium-conversion-rate-saas); [Guru Startups](https://www.gurustartups.com/reports/freemium-to-paid-conversion-rate-benchmarks)
- Free trials convert better than freemium: 8–12% is good and 15–25% is great — [Pulseahead](https://www.pulseahead.com/blog/trial-to-paid-conversion-benchmarks-in-saas); [ProductLed benchmarks](https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- **Pricing check.** Pro at 59,000 KRW is about US$40–43. That is roughly Yelp Verified License (about US$30) plus a small tools bundle, which is defensible. Growth at 149,000 KRW is about US$105–110. That is **below half of Weave Base or Vetstoria Small**, so there is room to go up for multi-location or clinic tiers if CRM and reminders are genuinely delivered.
- **Conversion math (illustrative, not a forecast).** Suppose 펫안심365 seeds 20,000 free public-data profiles, many unclaimed. The freemium benchmarks apply to *active* free users, not to scraped listings. A realistic funnel is profile → **claimed** (single-digit to low-double-digit %) → paid (2–5% of claimed with self-serve; 5–10% with sales assist). **Hundreds, not thousands,** of paying partners in year 1 is the plausible order of magnitude.
- **Rover and Wag's 20–40% take rates** apply to marketplaces that transact the service. 펫안심365 is an information and trust layer, so subscription or lead pricing fits better than a take rate.

### Gaps
- No published verified-listing **conversion rates** from Yelp or Google.
- Google Business Profile verification process and pricing were not fetched. OpenView and Lenny's Newsletter benchmarks were not retrieved, so the consultancy figures above are used instead.
- No PetDesk or Weave penetration or churn data found.

## 4. Korean legal constraints: veterinary advertising, review manipulation, ad labeling

### Takeaway
The rules below rule out several product features. **Vet clinics** cannot run false or exaggerated ads, claim unrecognized specialist titles, or offer **discounts through external platforms**, which counts as patient inducement; licenses can be suspended for 15 days to 6 months. A pre-review bill for vet ads is pending. **All endorsements** (blogs, SNS, creator content) must disclose the economic relationship **in the title or first part** (from 2024-12-01). The 2026-06-01 revision adds AI virtual-influencer labels. 공정위 actively pursues paid reviews, coupon-ad bundles and fraudulent ad agencies.

### Cited Findings
**수의사법 / 시행령 (veterinary advertising)**
- **Presidential Decree No. 34737, effective 2024-07-24,** moved advertising violations out of the "과잉진료" category into a separate provision on conduct damaging veterinary dignity (시행령 제20조의2) — [CND LOGIC](https://cndlogic.com/insights/animal-hospital-advertising-law); [데일리벳](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/practice/companion-animal/158851)
- Prohibited conduct includes:
  - False or exaggerated ads, including unrecognized "전문의" titles and unverifiable case counts
  - **Patient inducement:** social-commerce procedure discounts, card-partnership blanket 10% discounts and **fee reductions mediated by external platforms**
  - Letting non-vets take part in care
  - Using expired drugs
  - License suspension is **15 days for a 1st violation, 1 month for a 2nd and 6 months for a 3rd** within 12 months — [CND LOGIC](https://cndlogic.com/insights/animal-hospital-advertising-law)
- **Pending bill (2025):** 서삼석 의원's 수의사법 amendment would ban false or exaggerated ads and disparagement of other clinics. It would also **require pre-review (사전 심의)** of vet ads, with fines up to **3M KRW**. The National Assembly agriculture committee raised concerns about a single review body. Status was pending as of the sources found — [데일리벳](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/policy/239128); [네이트뉴스 2025-03-08](https://news.nate.com/view/20250308n05299); [한국동물병원협회 성명](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/policy/239337)
- MAFRA's transparency reforms:
  - Mandatory pre-treatment fee explanation
  - Posted-fee items expanded from 11 to 20
  - Standard care guides for 100 common conditions
  - VAT exemptions
  - A specialist certification system — [CND LOGIC](https://cndlogic.com/insights/animal-hospital-advertising-law) (secondary summary)
- 표시광고법 also applies to vet ads, so a clinic can face both a license suspension and 공정위 sanctions — [CND LOGIC](https://cndlogic.com/insights/animal-hospital-advertising-law)
- A court ruled that 의료법 does not apply to animal-hospital names — [데일리개원](https://www.dailygaewon.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=11618)

**추천·보증 심사지침 (endorsement/ad labeling)**
- **Revision effective 2024-12-01:**
  - On text media (blogs, cafés), the economic-interest disclosure must be in the **title or first part** of the post
  - Disclosure is required for cash, free product, vouchers, points, discounts, or being employed by the advertiser — [FTC 행정예고](https://www.ftc.go.kr/www/selectBbsNttView.do?key=12&bordCd=3&nttSn=47317); [김·장](https://www.kimchang.com/ko/insights/detail.kc?sch_section=4&idx=30241); [Daum 카페 공지](https://m.cafe.daum.net/supporters/MbmU/247)
- **Revision effective 2026-06-01:**
  - Posts with **AI virtual influencers** must be labeled, e.g. "AI를 기반으로 생성된 가상인물이 포함된 게시물입니다" or "가상인물 포함," in the title or opening, or next to the character in video
  - Experience-based claims made by virtual people must still be factual — [공정위 보도자료](https://www.ftc.go.kr/www/selectBbsNttView.do?pageUnit=10&pageIndex=1&searchCnd=all&key=12&bordCd=3&searchCtgry=01%2C02&nttSn=47547)
- 공정위 published a Q&A guide on economic-interest disclosure on 2025-12-02 **[secondary]** — [Clean Work Lab](https://cleanworklab.com/posts/influencer-ad-disclosure/); [FTC 안내서](https://www.ftc.go.kr/www/selectBbsNttView.do?bordCd=3&key=12&nttSn=46709&searchCtgry=01,02)
- Claiming experience you did not have, or presenting an expert endorsement as professional judgment when it is not, can count as false or exaggerated advertising — [찾기쉬운 생활법령](https://easylaw.go.kr/CSP/CnpClsMain.laf?popMenu=ov&csmSeq=1575&ccfNo=2&cciNo=3&cnpClsNo=1)

**Enforcement climate (review/ranking/ad sales)**
- 야놀자/여기어때: 15.4B KRW in surcharges plus a 2026 prosecutor raid over ad-coupon bundles (see Q2) — [법률신문](https://www.lawtimes.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=210486)
- 2026-07: sanctions on three plastic-surgery clinics over undisclosed paid reviews (see Q2) — [네이트뉴스](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260712n06493)
- 2026-05: 공정위's online ad-agency fraud TF referred **18 agencies** to police for targeting small businesses, often by posing as "government-support" ad programs — [아시아경제 2026-05-12](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026051210020899388)
- 공정위 has also revised its deceptive-advertising review guidelines (기만적 표시·광고 심사지침) — [FTC](https://www.ftc.go.kr/www/selectBbsNttView.do?key=12&bordCd=3&nttSn=46562)

### Inferences
- **Product rules for 펫안심365:**
  1. Paid tiers must **not** change safety scores or organic ranking, or they risk being deceptive. Show a clear "광고" or "파트너" label on any sponsored slot.
  2. Growth-tier coupons **cannot be offered for vet treatment** (patient-inducement risk under 시행령 20조의2). Limit coupons to grooming, hotel, café and goods, or to non-clinical clinic items after legal review.
  3. Review replies are fine. Any review incentive, such as points for reviews, needs disclosure, and the platform should never sell review removal.
  4. Creator-led education and sponsored content need the "광고/협찬" disclosure in the title or first line. AI-generated presenters need a virtual-person label (from 2026-06-01).
  5. Vet partner ads should be pre-screened internally against 시행령 20조의2, since mandatory pre-review may become law.
- The "verified badge" must say exactly what was verified: license, business registration, public-data match, or an on-site check. That avoids 표시광고법 exposure from implying a quality endorsement.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether the 2025 수의사법 pre-review bill passed by Sep 2026.
- No specific 공정위 case found on **ranking-for-sale in a map or listing app** (e.g., "추천순" undisclosed ads) for 2024–2026. That case law may exist but was not retrieved.
- The legal status of platforms charging **per-lead fees to animal hospitals** under 수의사법 is not directly addressed in the sources. The human-medicine analog (의료법 27조) is clear, but the veterinary rule is untested.

## 5. Recommended pricing ladders, alternative revenue lines, and first-30-partner pilot tactics

### Takeaway
The evidence supports a **free claimed profile**, a Pro plan at about **39,000–59,000 KRW**, and a Growth plan at about **149,000 KRW**. Clinic and multi-location plans can go higher because comparable global SaaS costs 270K–680K KRW. The launch should rely on **founding-partner discounts and done-for-you onboarding** rather than self-serve conversion. There is little direct evidence on Korean pilot tactics, so most of this section is inference.

### Cited Findings (price anchors assembled from above)
- Korean flat-fee anchor: 배민 울트라콜 at 88,000 KRW/month per flag (discontinued from 2025) — [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025013162291)
- Korean vet SaaS anchor: 플러스벳 at 0 / 22,000 / 44,000 KRW per vet per month, with 22,000 KRW add-ons — [플러스벳](https://vetching.cc/pricing)
- Verification-badge anchor: Yelp Verified License at US$1/day, about +10% engagement (company-reported) — [Yelp IR](https://www.yelp-ir.com/news/press-releases/news-release-details/2019/Yelp-Announces-Verified-Licenses-For-Service-Providers/default.aspx)
- Clinic comms anchors: Weave at US$199–497/month; Vetstoria at US$230–462/month — [Emitrr](https://emitrr.com/blog/weave-pricing/); [Vetstoria](https://www.vetstoria.com/pricing/)
- Local ad-spend anchors:
  - Vet Naver search at about 300–500K KRW/month **[agency]** — [더상승마케팅](https://increasemarketing.co.kr/animal-hospital-marketing/)
  - Accommodation-app average ad spend of 1.08M KRW/month (2024) — [아시아경제](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2024070316214179981)
- Conversion anchors: freemium 2–5% self-serve, 5–7% sales-assisted — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-freemium-conversion-rates/)
- Public-data and B2G signals:
  - 한국문화정보원 runs an open dataset of pet-friendly cultural facilities (built 2022, updated 2025-03-24) — [공공데이터포털](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15111389/fileData.do)
  - MAFRA publishes pet-business premises data, e.g. 장묘업 as of 2024-12-31 — [공공데이터포털](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15073587/fileData.do)
  - The government runs a "반려동물 연관산업 맞춤형 정보조사" program: company recruitment in 2025, and the survey work itself is tendered as a procurement contract — [기업마당](https://www.bizinfo.go.kr/web/lay1/bbs/S1T122C128/AS/74/view.do?pblancId=PBLN_000000000109565); [비드프로 입찰](https://www.pro1.co.kr/n0_ipchal/konggo/svc/konggo_view.asp?num=2852312)
  - Petting-venue search has no dominant player **[project-pitch source; weak]** — [렛플](https://letspl.me/project/2748/)

### Inferences (recommendations, not sourced facts)
- **Suggested ladder:**
  - **Basic (free, always visible)**: the public-data profile plus a "claim" flow. Claiming unlocks hours, photos and replies to 3 reviews a month. Don't charge for existence, given the 강남언니 2M KRW backlash.
  - **Pro at 49,000–59,000 KRW/month**, or about 490,000–590,000 KRW a year with two months free. Includes the verified badge (with a stated scope), inquiries/leads inbox, unlimited review replies and stats. Consider a **"Verified only" add-on at about 19,000–29,000 KRW**, benchmarked to Yelp's US$1/day and 플러스벳's 22,000 KRW add-ons, for price-sensitive groomers.
  - **Growth at 149,000 KRW/month**: CRM, Kakao 브랜드메시지 or alimtalk sends (pass through per-message cost at 23–35 KRW, or include a quota), merchant-funded coupons for non-clinical categories only, and multi-location at +39,000–59,000 KRW per extra site.
  - **Clinic SaaS (separate SKU)**: don't compete with EMR vendors. Integrate with them, e.g. a Naver 예약-style add-on at about 22,000–44,000 KRW, or a reminder and safety-notice module. Global vet comms prices suggest 150–300K KRW is possible later, but Korean EMR prices are much lower.
- **Alternative revenue lines** (ranked by evidence of willingness to pay and fit):
  1. **B2G / public-sector contracts**: pet-friendly-place data, safety-information services, municipal pet-safety campaigns. The government already tenders related survey and data work.
  2. **Sponsored content labeled "광고"**, from pet food, insurance and supplies brands rather than vets. This avoids the 수의사법 risk and follows the title/first-line disclosure rule.
  3. **Data and insight reports** on local pet-service supply, safety incidents and price transparency for insurers, brands and municipalities.
  4. **Creator-led education** (paid courses or B2B training for groomers and hotel staff on safety), with endorsement disclosures.
  5. Clinic SaaS as an integration layer.
- **First-30-partner pilot tactics** (inferred from benchmarks, not from a documented Korean case):
  - Choose **one district** with a dense mix of groomers, hotels, cafés and 1–2 clinics.
  - Offer **"Founding Partner" pricing**: 3 months free, then 50% off Pro locked for 12 months, in exchange for a testimonial or case study. The case-study consent must be disclosed if it is used as an endorsement.
  - Do the verification visit and profile setup yourself. Benchmarks show sales-assisted conversion is roughly double self-serve.
  - Report **monthly leads and inquiries against the equivalent Naver CPC cost** (e.g., "12 inquiries ≈ 60,000 KRW of Place clicks") to justify renewal.
  - Target categories with the highest safety anxiety and weakest Naver differentiation, such as **pet hotels/boarding and funeral**, before vet clinics, which carry more legal complexity and already have entrenched EMR relationships.
  - Set a success bar of **30 paid or trial partners and ≥50% converting to paid at month 4**, in line with the trial benchmarks of 8–25% at scale, where hand-picked pilots should do much better.

### Gaps
- No documented Korean pilot playbook for a comparable pet or local verified-listing launch. The tactics above are reasoned, not sourced.
- No data on Korean SMB willingness to pay specifically for **verification badges**.
- No sourced B2G contract values for municipal pet-information platforms. Only the existence of related tenders and datasets was confirmed.
- No sourced prices or market for pet-industry data reports or creator-education revenue in Korea.
