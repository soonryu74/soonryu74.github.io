# Korean Pet Service O2O, Pet-Friendly Place/Travel, Community, and Lost/Adoption/Abuse Platforms (as of Sept 2026)

Scope note: research done 2026-09-25 via web search/fetch. Years are labeled. Items not confirmed by a source are marked **[UNVERIFIED]** or listed in Gaps. Named in the brief but NOT found in any source: "페트몬", "펫츠고", "코리아 펫 트래블", "도그데이" (as a pet SNS). "펫스타그램" appears to be an Instagram hashtag culture, not a standalone platform (no source found for an app by that name).

## 1. Service O2O (pet sitting / walking / grooming / boarding): existence, status, model, traction, failure reasons

### Takeaway
The pioneer home-sitter platform 도그메이트 (2015) went into court rehabilitation and was sold in 2025 after COVID-era revenue collapse and unpaid debt. 펫피플 (와요 visit-sitting plus 펫플래닛 home boarding) is the surviving leader. 펫플래닛's home boarding is legal only under a regulatory-sandbox special exemption. Much real demand has moved to unregistered, uninsured individual care arranged through 당근 at about half the price of licensed pet hotels, and accident compensation there is effectively a legal blind spot. In grooming, the market has shifted from consumer booking marketplaces to shop-side SaaS (반짝, 그루머노트, 포슬, 포포넛 비즈니스).

### Cited Findings
**도그메이트 (Dogmate): distressed, sold**
- Founded 2015 as a platform matching dog owners with home pet sitters ("가정집 펫시터"). Cumulative stats: 225,680 sitting transactions, 57,366 registered sitters, 46,522 dogs. Accumulated deficit ₩4.4B; current liabilities ₩1.3B vs liquid assets ₩58.75M (Oct 2024); capital ₩3.1B. Series A of ₩2.4B in 2020 (Strong Ventures, GS Retail and others). It entered 회생절차 (court rehabilitation) because low revenue during COVID-19 left it unable to repay borrowings. Pre-approval M&A was run by Hyundai Accounting, with bids closing 2025-03-28. — [Bloter, 2025-03](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=634031); [Nate/Bloter](https://news.nate.com/view/20250330n16549)
- 커넥팅더닷츠 (operator of kids-care platform 째깍악어) acquired 도그메이트 in a rehabilitation M&A (reported 2025-06-10), framed as building an "integrated care ecosystem". It is described as having run for 10 years before management difficulties. — [와우테일, 2025-06-10](https://wowtale.net/2025/06/10/242228/) (page returned 403 on fetch; summary taken from the search snippet); [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/dogmate)
- A mid-2025 consumer guide still listed 도그메이트 as active and offering a ₩10,000 first-visit trial. — [리앤폴, updated 2025-08-27](https://leeandpol.com/ko/pet-sitter/)

**펫피플: 와요 (WAYO) and 펫플래닛 (PetPlanet), active**
- 펫피플 runs two services: 펫플래닛 (home boarding / 위탁 sitter) and 와요 (in-home visit sitter). Features include hourly photo/video care logs, 1:1 chat and in-app live video streaming. It raised investment from Naver-affiliated Spring Camp in 2019. It reported 1,578% YoY growth in active users (Aug 2019), with one month's GMV exceeding all of 2018 (2019 self-reported). — [Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/125589); [Venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/786709)
- 와요 (launched 2019) claims 100,000+ downloads and a 4.9 rating. Sitter acceptance rate is "10%대", 2-hour emergency visits are offered, and 30/60/120-minute visits are sold. Service areas are Seoul/capital region plus Busan, Daegu, Daejeon and Gwangju. It also offers trainers, groomers and a pet-friendly place search. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.petpeople.wayo&hl=en_US); [wayopet.com](https://wayopet.com/); [와요 places](https://wayopet.com/places)
- 펫플래닛 states it operates legally as a 규제샌드박스 실증특례 (regulatory sandbox special exemption) approved by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE). — [petplanet.co](https://petplanet.co/)
- Other active sitting apps: 펫봄 (neighbor sitter), 포캣/페팸 (cat visit sitting). — [petbom.com](https://petbom.com/); [Google Play 포캣](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.moriahtown.pefamcat&hl=en_US)
- Market prices (2025): a 1-hour visit costs ₩20,000–30,000, twice-daily visits ₩50,000–80,000 per day, overnight care about ₩30,000–70,000, with regional variation. No platform discloses its commission. — [리앤폴 2025](https://leeandpol.com/ko/pet-sitter/)

**Structural and legal problems (why home-sitter O2O is fragile)**
- Under current law, a detached house or apartment cannot be registered as a 동물위탁관리업 premises, and there is no business category for recurring paid home sitting via platforms. Home boarding therefore sits in a legal gray area. — [TheFrontier/스타트업포레스트](https://thefrontier.co.kr/category-regulation-pet/); [경인일보](https://www.kyeongin.com/article/1771453)
- (2026-09) Only 5,803 of 24,384 pet businesses (23.8%) are registered 동물위탁관리업. Unregistered repeat home care is illegal (fine up to ₩5M). Individual caregivers carry no mandatory liability insurance.
  - Pricing: registered pet hotels charge ₩40,000–60,000 per night for small dogs, with a 10–20% holiday surcharge. Individual care via 당근 averages about ₩25,000 per visit, sometimes ₩10,000.
  - 당근 pet-care posts rose 3.5x in the two weeks before the holiday. 당근 restricted pet-care listings after disputes, including owners who went missing after dropping off animals.
  - The article cites a pet lost during a platform-arranged walk where no insurance applied.
  - Source: [매일신문, 2026-09-21](https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092115185475165)
- Pet hotel and grooming accidents (e.g., a dog nearly blinded two hours after drop-off) lack clear dispute-resolution standards. Conflicts escalate to online exposés and lawsuits. — [한국경제, 2024-02](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202402067820Y)
- Owners must prove the operator breached its duty of care to win damages. The 동물보호법 requires CCTV, but footage is often missing or kept too briefly to serve as evidence. — [머니투데이, 2026-05-02](https://www.mt.co.kr/society/2026/05/02/2026042914361946364)
- (2019) 한국경제 reported home pet sitters earning up to ₩10M per month in a "management blind spot". — [한국경제 2019](https://www.hankyung.com/society/article/201909036977i)

**Grooming / hotel booking: shop-side SaaS dominates**
- 반짝 (펫이지) calls itself Korea's #1 grooming booking and customer-management service. It is shop-facing, with a separate "반짝 미용파트너" app. Features: bookings, automatic reminders including the day before (framed as no-show prevention), e-consent forms, sales stats, prepaid package and membership management, and daycare/hotel management. It planned hotel booking, treats, health check-ups and insurance. It raised investment from 네오플라이. Pricing is not disclosed, and the partner-count/download fields on the site read 0 at fetch time (placeholder). — [Venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/796978); [banjjakpet.com](https://www.banjjakpet.com/); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.banjjakpet.beautypartner&hl=ko)
- Other shop tools: 그루머노트 (bookings, customers, sales, post-grooming notes, 카카오 알림톡, consent forms), 포슬 (all-in-one incl. repeat-visit and regular-customer management), 포포넛 비즈니스 (grooming shops, daycares, hotels). Consumer-side booking concepts include 댕컷 and 복슬복슬 (matching fee plus ads model; early-stage). — [groomernote.com](https://groomernote.com/); [posle.kr](https://posle.kr/); [App Store 포포넛](https://apps.apple.com/hn/app/%ED%8F%AC%ED%8F%AC%EB%84%9B-%EB%B9%84%EC%A6%88%EB%8B%88%EC%8A%A4/id6743143732?l=es-MX); [letspl.me 복슬복슬](https://letspl.me/project/895/)
- Demand context (2025 survey, published 2026-02-12): among owners, 95.1% used a vet in the past year, 50.8% used grooming, 35.5% pet parks and 12.9% pet hotels. — [농식품부 보도자료 2026-02-12](https://www.mafra.go.kr/bbs/home/792/576961/artclView.do)

### Inferences
- Home-sitter O2O suffers from low frequency (holidays and travel), thin take rates, high trust and insurance costs, and legal gray zones. COVID travel collapse was fatal for 도그메이트. Informal C2C (당근) undercuts on price. A safety-first platform should not enter sitter matching directly. It can instead differentiate on verification and incident guidance: checking 위탁관리업 registration, whether CCTV is kept, insurance status, and what to do after an accident.
- Grooming and hotel shops already buy booking/CRM SaaS, whose pitch centers on no-shows. A "verified partner profile" that competes with that SaaS will struggle. One that complements it (a trust badge plus a link-out to the shop's Naver/반짝 booking page) is more plausible.
- Only 23.8% of care businesses are registered for 위탁관리업. Public registration status is therefore a meaningful and checkable trust signal for 펫안심365's facility map. The public 동물 영업 등록 data source was not verified here.

### Gaps
- No disclosed commission rates for 와요, 펫플래닛, 펫봄, 반짝 or 당근 (none found).
- 펫피플 revenue and profit for 2024/2025 not found (innoforest/THE VC pages exist but figures were not retrieved).
- "페트몬" not found in any source; it may not exist or may be a misname. Post-acquisition status of 도그메이트 under 커넥팅더닷츠 (2026) is not confirmed.
- No verified count of 반짝 partner shops.

## 2. Pet-friendly places and travel (maps, OTAs, KTO/TourAPI, restaurant regulation)

### Takeaway
Pet-friendly place data in Korea is fragmented. Sources include self-registered OTA filters, Naver map keyword/campaign pages, KTO TourAPI data (about 260,000 records of pet-travel info) and startups such as 반려생활 (30,000 places). All rely largely on operator self-declaration and warn users to call ahead. From 2026-03-01, restaurants and cafes may legally admit dogs and cats only if they file a 반려동물 동반 영업 notification and meet hygiene standards. This creates a new official, verifiable status that most existing maps do not yet reflect.

### Cited Findings
**Restaurant regulation (food hygiene law enforcement rule, 식품위생법 시행규칙)**
- The Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS, 식약처) pre-announced (입법예고) a 식품위생법 시행규칙 amendment creating a legal basis and hygiene/safety standards for pet entry to restaurants. It took effect **2026-03-01**, replacing an earlier 시범사업 (pilot program) under 규제 개선. — [정책브리핑](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148942466); [식약처 규제개선 시범사업](https://www.mfds.go.kr/usr/rgltnInvtView.do?seq=202300037)
- Only dogs and cats are allowed, and participation is voluntary. The operator must file a "반려동물 동반 영업 신고/통보".
  - Required facilities: partitions or fences keeping animals out of kitchens and storage; hand sanitizer at the entrance; entrance signage marking the shop as pet-allowed; a "no unvaccinated pets" notice; dedicated seating or leash hooks; table spacing; covered food; separate pet dishes; a waste bin for animal waste.
  - Violations bring administrative sanctions from corrective orders up to business suspension. The government recommends liability insurance.
  - Source: [데일리벳, 2026-01-03](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/etc/269101); [Nate 2026-01-02](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260102n17891); [Nate 2026-03-04](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260304n32316)
- Correction to the brief: the amendment was pre-announced in 2025 but **took effect 2026-03-01**, not in 2025. — [데일리벳](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/etc/269101)

**Maps**
- Naver (2023-04) launched the "갈수있어 강아지도" campaign page listing dog-friendly restaurants, cafes, malls and lodging by region. Examples: Jeju 786, Busan 261, Gyeongbuk 265, Jeonbuk 300, Chungbuk 159, and roughly 650 per Seoul area. Users search keywords such as "애견동반식당" in Naver Map. The article cites Naver Map at 21.55M monthly users vs Kakao Map at 9.47M. How the data was sourced was not stated. — [서울경제, 2023-04-09](https://www.sedaily.com/article/13640027)
- Kakao Map shows "반려동물 동반 가능" on some place pages. A developer forum says there is no public API or filter parameter for it. — [Kakao DevTalk](https://devtalk.kakao.com/t/api/151063)
- Independent maps include 델고 (user-built dog map), 어디가개, 로카펫, 비마이펫 and 위드펫 (위드펫 is built on KTO data). — [App Store 델고](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1662427735); [dogswhere.com](https://dogswhere.com/map); [withpet-tani](https://www.withpet-tani.com/tourist-spots); [bemypet](https://bemypet.kr/pet-places/3895)

**Accommodation / OTA**
- 야놀자 (now NOL) runs pet-friendly lodging exhibitions (e.g., Jeju pet pensions, pet hotels and resorts). Through a 2021 partnership with 반려생활 it provided real-time booking at about 1,100 pet-friendly lodgings. — [NOL 기획전](https://nol.yanolja.com/exhibition/7111); [Venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/852099)
- Airbnb Korea reports sharply rising searches for pet-friendly stays and has partnered with KTO and Jeonbuk local governments on pet travel. — [Airbnb News](https://news.airbnb.com/ko/kto-pet-friendly-travel/)
- 여기어때 was not specifically verified beyond a generic pet-filter mention in search snippets **[UNVERIFIED]**.

**반려생활 (Ban-Life) and KTO**
- 반려생활 was founded in 2019 by 이혜미, an ex-배달의민족 developer. It has 200,000 users and 860,000 cumulative downloads (2026-03), and lists 30,000 places across 12 categories.
  - Revenue has three parts: B2C booking commission, B2G packages with KTO and local governments, and B2B advertising/sponsored content.
  - Funding: seed round from Spring Camp (2020-05), TIPS selection (2021-06).
  - The founder's stated problem is that pet-friendly information was "severely insufficient" and fragmented, forcing manual phone checks.
  - Source: [아시아경제, 2026-03-26](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026032509145367510)
- KTO, 반려생활 and HiAir ran a pet charter flight product (Gimpo–Jeju, 2 nights/3 days, monthly, 10 runs). — [KTO/뉴스1 snippet](https://www.news1.kr/industry/hotel-tourism/5669061)
- KTO's TourAPI provides "반려동물 동반여행정보 15종 약 26만 건" (Korean language), including the `detailPetTour2` endpoint. It is open via data.go.kr. — [공공데이터포털](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15101578/openapi.do); [한국관광콘텐츠랩](https://api.visitkorea.or.kr/)
- 2026 KTO/Kakao 관광데이터 공모전 entries include pet-travel matching (e.g., "PetPass"). This shows TourAPI pet data is easy for newcomers to build on, so it is not a moat. — [GitHub petpass](https://github.com/UnohNam/petpass); [2025tourapi.com](https://www.2025tourapi.com/)
- MCST and KTO published a "반려동물 동반여행 친화시설 조성 가이드라인" (2025-10). It covers tourist sites, lodging and F&B, with required items (pet disinfectant, leash fixtures), recommended items (lounges, play areas, toilet areas), checklists and manual templates. KTO has designated 6 반려동물 친화 관광도시 since 2022: Ulsan, Taean, Pocheon, Suncheon, Iksan and Gyeongju. — [아시아경제, 2025-10-13](https://www.asiae.co.kr/article/2025101313363856601); [외식관광뉴스](http://www.ft365.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4794)

### Inferences
- The 2026-03-01 restaurant rule gives 펫안심365 a clear verification rubric: filed notification, entrance signage, partitions and a vaccination notice. A "legally registered pet-allowed restaurant" label differs from "user-reported pet-friendly". Whether a public list of notified restaurants exists needs checking (see Gaps).
- KTO's 친화시설 guideline checklist could serve as the verification standard for partner badges, borrowing government legitimacy.
- Incumbents (Naver and Kakao maps, OTAs, 반려생활) own discovery and booking. 펫안심365's defensible angle is verification status, freshness and safety attributes (size/breed limits, vaccination requirements, nearest 24h vet). Breadth of listings is not defensible.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of a current dedicated "반려동물 동반" filter in Naver Map (2025–26) or of how Naver/Kakao source the attribute (e.g., Smart Place owner self-entry). **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Not confirmed whether MFDS or local governments publish a searchable list or open data of restaurants that filed pet-entry notifications, or how many filed after 2026-03-01.
- "코리아 펫 트래블" and "펫츠고" were not found.

## 3. Lost/adoption/abuse reporting, communities, and registration data

### Takeaway
Official channels are fragmented. Lost and found animals are posted on 국가동물보호정보시스템 (animal.go.kr) for a short notice period (about 7–10 days, after which ownership passes to the local government). Abuse goes to 112, local governments (120), or NGOs such as 동물자유연대, whose "동바고" tool drafts complaints. Outcomes are poor: in 2024 only 11.4% of 106,824 lost or abandoned animals returned home (dogs 15.1%, cats 1.3%), and 46% died in shelters. 포인핸드 is the dominant adoption and lost-pet app, with web launched 2025-09 and 10,000+ adoptions per year. It is donation-funded after abandoning commerce.

### Cited Findings
**Lost/abandoned outcomes (2024 data, published 2025-06)**
- 2024 lost and abandoned animals totaled 106,824 (−5.5% YoY): dogs 77,304 (72.4%), cats 27,826 (26.0%), other 1,694.
  - Outcomes: returned to owner 11.4% (12,188), adopted 23.5%, natural death 27.5%, euthanasia 18.5%.
  - Return rate is 15.1% for dogs and 1.3% for cats. Adoption has fallen 3 years running from 32.1% in 2021.
  - Shelters: 263 (231 registered), 999 staff. Operating budget ₩46.42B (+24.2%), ₩435,000 per animal.
  - Source: [데일리벳, 2025-06-04](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/policy/248072); [KDI/농식품부 2025-05-27](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=266993)
- 2023 data for comparison: 29.8% natural death and 21.8% euthanasia (51.6% died in shelters). Among animals under 1 year old, 59.3% died. — [한국일보, 2024-07-01](https://www.hankookilbo.com/News/Read/A2024070116230001688)
- The notice period is about 7–10 days, after which ownership transfers to the local government under the 동물보호법. — [쿠키뉴스, 2023-08](https://www.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202308040066)

**Registration**
- Cumulative registered dogs and cats reached 3.49M by end-2024 (+6.3%). New registrations in 2024 were 260,000 (−4.2%): 245,000 dogs and 15,000 cats. Pet businesses number 24,000 (+14.5%). — [KDI/농식품부 2025-05-27](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=266993)
- 2024 survey: awareness of the registration system was 63.6% (+7.5pp), and 76.4% of dog owners said they had registered (self-reported). Adoption from shelters rose from 8.9% to 12.2%. Average monthly cost was ₩142,000 (2024 survey). — [농식품부 2024 국민의식조사](https://www.mafra.go.kr/bbs/home/792/573057/artclView.do?layout=unknown); [KDI](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=262630)
- 2025 survey (published 2026-02-12): 29.2% of households own pets, a record, described as "3가구 중 1가구" (roughly 1 in 3 households); dogs 80.5%, cats 14.4%.
  - Awareness of animal welfare law and rules is 74.9%, but only 48.8% of dog owners say they comply with owner obligations. 93.2% support stronger abuse penalties.
  - Acquisition: from acquaintances 46.0%, pet shops 28.7%, shelter adoption 9.0%.
  - Monthly cost is ₩121,000 per animal (vet ₩37,000).
  - Method: 3,000 households, in-person, fieldwork 2025-10-31 to 2025-12-12; plus a 5,000-person online survey.
  - Source: [농식품부 2026-02-12](https://www.mafra.go.kr/bbs/home/792/576961/artclView.do); [데일리벳](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/policy/273697)
- Data conflict to flag: monthly cost was ₩142,000 in the 2024 survey but ₩121,000 in the 2025 survey, and shelter adoption was 12.2% in 2024 vs 9.0% in 2025. Methods may differ; the 2025 household survey was in-person. Do not treat these as a trend without checking methodology.

**포인핸드 (PawInHand)**
- Founded 2013-11 by veterinarian 이환희, inspired by his public-health vet service at a municipal shelter. It aggregates adoption listings from shelters nationwide and supports lost-pet search.
  - Web version launched 2025-09-24, usable without installing the app.
  - Brokers "연간 1만 마리 이상" adoptions per year.
  - Tried commerce ("포인핸드 몰") but dropped it as off-mission. It is now funded by subscription and one-off donations and corporate gifts (e.g., 포포즈 ₩300M), and has an offline 경의숲점 location.
  - Source: [국민일보 2025-09](https://www.kmib.co.kr/article/view_amp.asp?arcid=0028738761); [서울신문 2021](https://www.seoul.co.kr/news/plan/person/2021/09/29/20210929014005); [포인핸드 후원](https://pawinhand.kr/service/sponsorship.html); [굿모닝경제](https://www.goodkyung.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=264929); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1019549518)

**Abuse reporting channels**
- Recommended escalation: police 112 first; if police don't respond, the local government's dedicated officer (동물보호감시원, or 120); NGOs such as 한국동물보호협회, 케어 and 동물자유연대 (02-2292-6337); 카라 accepts email tips (info@ekara.org). — [동물자유연대 대응 매뉴얼](https://www.animals.or.kr/campaign/friend/52555); [카라](https://ekara.org/activity/policy/read/14328); [aboutpet](https://aboutpet.co.kr/eventNew/detail?eventNo=420)
- 동바고 (abuse.animals.or.kr), associated with 동물자유연대 by domain, offers an abuse self-check, complaint (고발장) drafting, response tips and a database of case law by offense type. Operator details and usage counts were not retrievable. — [동바고](https://abuse.animals.or.kr/guide)
- Enforcement is weak. Of 4,601 animal-protection cases in 2020–2024, 45.1% were prosecuted. Of 416 first-instance convictions, 7.0% resulted in imprisonment and 68.8% in fines. — [매일신문, 2026-09-21](https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092115185475165)

**Communities**
- 고양이라서 다행이야 (고다), a Naver café founded 2003-12-11, is described as having 600,000–700,000 members and being one of Naver's representative cafés. ## 4. Where platforms fail on trust and safety

### Takeaway
The recurring failures are these. Pet-friendly status is self-registered and hides size, breed, species and fee conditions, and goes stale. Care accidents have no clear liability standard, CCTV evidence is often unavailable, and informal sitters are uninsured. Most care businesses are not registered for 위탁관리업. Guides universally tell users to "call to confirm," which is a direct opening for verified status plus last-checked dates.

### Cited Findings
- OTA pet filters rely on the property's self-registration. Hidden conditions include small dogs only, one pet maximum, no cats or large breeds, undisclosed extra fees, and outdated policies. Users are told to phone ahead and check #펫동반숙소 UGC. — [여행픽, 2025-06-20](https://www.thetrippick.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=199)
- The 반려생활 founder describes information as insufficient and fragmented, requiring phone verification (2026). — [아시아경제 2026-03-26](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026032509145367510)
- Care accidents lack dispute standards and escalate to online exposés and lawsuits (2024). Proving negligence is hard, and CCTV is often absent or kept too briefly (2026). — [한국경제 2024-02](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202402067820Y); [머니투데이 2026-05](https://www.mt.co.kr/society/2026/05/02/2026042914361946364)
- Only 23.8% of care businesses are registered for 위탁관리업. Informal 당근 sitters have no mandatory insurance. 당근 restricted pet-care listings after abandonment disputes (2026). — [매일신문 2026-09-21](https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092115185475165)
- Local-business ad agencies use aggressive tactics, and owners report spending around 30% of weekly revenue. This bears on the credibility of paid placement and reviews (2023). — [머니투데이 2023-04-17](https://www.mt.co.kr/industry/2023/04/17/2023041709141547934)

### Inferences
- Trust signals 펫안심365 could show:
  - official registration (위탁관리업 or 미용업 business registration; the restaurant pet-entry notification from 2026-03)
  - a last-verified date
  - explicit size, species and vaccination conditions
  - insurance held (yes/no)
  - CCTV retention policy
  - a clear separation of paid partner status from verification status, to avoid the "paid equals trusted" perception.
- An "unverified" label is well-motivated. The market's own advice ("call first") shows that users already assume listings are unreliable.

### Gaps
- No quantitative fake-review data specific to pet businesses in Korea found.
- No 한국소비자원 statistics on pet hotel, grooming or sitter complaints for 2024–26 were retrieved (worth a follow-up search).

## 5. Partner business pain points and willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Local pet businesses already pay for acquisition and operations: Naver Place ads (per-click, capped daily) and sometimes costly agencies, and booking/CRM SaaS whose headline value is no-show prevention via reminders and deposits. Pet-specific commission rates are not publicly disclosed. Willingness to pay is proven for acquisition and no-show tools, not for trust badges alone.

### Cited Findings
- Naver Place ads charge a fixed ₩55 per click when there are fewer than 10 competing advertisers, otherwise a bid price, with a daily cap of about ₩20,000. On the 아프니까 사장이다 café, owners report paying agencies around ₩500,000 per week (about 30% of weekly revenue) (2023). — [머니투데이 2023-04-17](https://www.mt.co.kr/industry/2023/04/17/2023041709141547934); [플레이스비서 2026 guide](https://ai.euncoach.com/guide/naver-place-ad-cost)
- Smart Place bookings can require a deposit to cut no-shows, but deposits complicate changes and refunds. Some small operators avoid deposits because of the admin burden. — [플레이스비서](https://ai.euncoach.com/guide/naver-place-ad-cost); [Threads post (anecdotal)](https://www.threads.com/@shiatsu_master/post/DG9A_cFzmOo)
- Grooming SaaS (반짝, 그루머노트, 포슬, 포포넛) sells automatic reminders, customer history, consent forms, prepaid packages and sales stats. Testimonials cite fewer no-shows. — [banjjakpet.com](https://www.banjjakpet.com/); [groomernote.com](https://groomernote.com/); [posle.kr](https://posle.kr/)
- 반려생활 monetizes B2B through advertising and sponsored content plus booking commission (2026). — [아시아경제 2026-03-26](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026032509145367510)
- Business base: 24,000 registered pet businesses (+14.5%, 2024) and 5,803 of 24,384 registered for 위탁관리업. — [KDI/농식품부](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=266993); [매일신문 2026-09](https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092115185475165)
- Hotel prices (₩40,000–60,000 per night) show licensed operators are undercut by informal ₩25,000 care. That gives licensed shops a motive to pay for a "registered and insured" differentiation badge. The article states the price gap; the willingness to pay is an inference. — [매일신문 2026-09](https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092115185475165)

### Inferences
- Pricing anchors for a verified partner profile: below a shop's Naver Place ad spend (hundreds of thousands of won per month at most), plausibly a low monthly fee or free verification with paid extras (priority placement, alerts, review responses).
- The strongest paid hook for licensed 위탁/호텔 operators is proof of legitimacy against informal competitors: registration, insurance and CCTV shown on a safety-branded platform, with safety content driving demand to them.
- 펫안심365 should integrate with incumbent booking (a Naver 예약 link, 반짝) rather than build booking, avoiding head-on SaaS competition.

### Gaps
- No disclosed commission rates for pet O2O or grooming booking platforms in Korea (2024–26).
- No survey of pet-business willingness to pay for verification or listing badges found.
- No data on how many pet shops use Naver Place ads or grooming SaaS.
