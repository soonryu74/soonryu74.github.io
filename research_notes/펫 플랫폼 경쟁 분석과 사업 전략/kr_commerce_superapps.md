# Korean Pet Commerce & "Super-App" Platforms: Competitor Profiles (research as of Sept 2026)

Scope note: Korea only. Figures are labeled with their year. Where a figure came from a search-result summary rather than a fetched article, it is marked "(search snippet)". Unverified items are marked **[UNVERIFIED]**. Research budget: ~25 tool calls; DART filings were not opened directly, so financials come from press coverage of those filings.

## Q1. Which platforms matter, and which exist and are active in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The pet-commerce field in 2025–2026 has consolidated around one profitable vertical leader, **펫프렌즈** (first-ever annual profit in 2025). **어바웃펫** is being sold by GS리테일 after years of losses. **핏펫** is in full capital impairment and looking for pre-IPO money. **바잇미** is a small but profitable D2C/brand player. Horizontal giants (쿠팡, 네이버) dominate overall e-commerce share. The "super-app" attempts from big corporates (LG U+ 포동; GS's pet businesses) have mostly stalled or are being wound down. Of the names in the brief, 펫생각 and 올웨이즈펫 could not be verified as pet platforms. 펫띵 exists as a subscription brand, but its current activity is unverified.

### Cited Findings

**펫프렌즈 (Pet Friends): the market leader, now profitable**
- Founded 2015. Acquired in 2021 jointly by IMM PE and GS리테일. GMV grew from 732억 (2021) to 1,515억 (2025), about 19.9% CAGR — [Venturesquare, 2026-03-12](https://www.venturesquare.net/1046147); [한국경제TV](https://www.wowtv.co.kr/NewsCenter/News/Read?articleId=A202603120230)
- 2025: GMV 1,515억 (+8.5% YoY), revenue 1,285억 (+9.8%), operating profit 5억, net profit 2억. This is the **first annual profit since founding**, and it is the first pet-commerce company to pass 1,500억 GMV — [Venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/1046147); [ACROFAN](https://kr.acrofan.com/detail.php?number=425564)
- 2024: revenue 1,171억 (+13.7%), operating loss 38억 (loss down 75%) — [Bloter/넘버스](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=635219) (search snippet)
- Ownership at end-2023: IMM PE (컴패니언1호) 65.6%, GS리테일 29.9%, founder 김창원 2.2%. IMM formally started a sale in late 2023 but has taken its time ("장고"). The fund (로즈골드 4호) matures in 2029. GS리테일 holds a right of first refusal (우선매수권) — [Bloter](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=635219); [딜사이트](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/137085/075023) (search snippet)
- Features and operations: data-driven personalized recommendations, the "집사생활" community, in-house logistics, and a high-margin advertising business. The company states plans to **expand into healthcare, insurance and content**. It reports roughly 85% annual repurchase rate — [Venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/1046147)
- Usage: H1 2023 average MAU of 320,143 (Mobile Index), #1 among pet apps with 37.6% share. Next were 포인핸드 at 168,805, 핏펫 at 94,737 and 어바웃펫 at 76,043 — [Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/210575); [노트펫](https://www.notepet.co.kr/news/article/article_view/?idx=28648). *(2023 data; no 2025 Mobile Index pet-app ranking found.)*

**어바웃펫 (aboutPet, GS리테일): loss-making, being sold**
- GS리테일 bought 24.66% of 펫츠비 (later 어바웃펫) for 50억 in 2018 and later raised its stake to 66.15%. In Jan 2023 GS paid 67억 for shares after the founders (나옥귀·강연진) exercised a put option — [Bizwatch 2023-03](http://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/consumer/2023/03/24/0025) (search snippet)
- 2024 revenue 284억, net loss 159억. Q1–Q3 2025 operating loss 75억. **Cumulative operating loss since the 2018 acquisition reached 734억** by Sept 2025 — [CEO스코어데일리](https://m.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2025022612005877400) (search snippet; the headline confirms 734억)
- Cumulative losses from 2019 to 2024 were about 760억. **Total equity was -187억 as of June 2025 (완전자본잠식)**. On 2025-10-14 the GS리테일 board approved forgiving 200억 of loans to 어바웃펫 and put it up for sale. The sale is framed as "선택과 집중" after GS25 lost the #1 convenience-store spot to CU in Q2 2025 — [한국금융신문](https://www.fntimes.com/html/view.php?ud=202510221137305563b5b890e35c_18); [전자신문](https://www.etnews.com/20251014000392); [뉴데일리](https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2025/10/14/2025101400409.html)
- Buyer: an industry source said the company is being sold "to the founder" — [스마트투데이](https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/93486). **[UNVERIFIED: no report of deal closing or the final buyer found as of Sept 2026.]**
- Features (as of about 2024): commerce plus content ("펫TV", including the training series "펫스쿨") plus care consultation, and the "로그" social feed. A "실비클럽" membership at 19,900원/month bundled pet insurance, vet consultation and up to 10% points — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/%EC%96%B4%EB%B0%94%EC%9B%83%ED%8E%AB-aboutpet/id1023281662); [모비인사이드 2024-03](https://www.mobiinside.co.kr/2024/03/21/petfriends-2/) (search snippet). **[UNVERIFIED whether 실비클럽 still runs in 2026.]**
- Bizwatch ran a 2025-04 piece titled "'만년 적자' 어바웃펫, 다시 살려낸 비결은," which suggests a turnaround narrative before the sale decision — [Bizwatch](http://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/consumer/2025/04/07/0044) (not read in full)

**핏펫 (Fitpet) / 어헤드: health-first "value chain" play in financial distress**
- Founded June 2017. It started from the at-home urine diagnostic kit "어헤드" (tests for diabetes, kidney and oral problems). It expanded into an app that stores results, health-linked commerce, supplements, distribution and logistics, **offline animal-hospital operation**, and insurance — [CCBS/toptvkorea 2026](https://www.toptvkorea.com/news/524316); [Daum/news 2026-05-11](https://v.daum.net/v/20260511090115291)
- In March 2024 it acquired 100% of 더블엔씨 (the gifticon resale app 니콘내콘, with about 440억 revenue in 2024). **Consolidated 2024 revenue was 687억 (vs 423억 in 2023), operating loss 136억 (vs 174억)** — [머니투데이/유니콘팩토리 2025-05](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2025/05/04/2025050211330580002). The standalone figure for 2024 is much lower, at revenue of about 163억 — [사람인](https://www.saramin.co.kr/zf_user/company-info/view-inner-finance/csn/eEhyTTFxWDJUUHg4WnRRRkdTS3ByUT09/company_nm/(%EC%A3%BC)%ED%95%8F%ED%8E%AB). *Most of the "growth" is non-pet gifticon revenue.*
- 2025: about 686억 consolidated revenue with continued operating losses — [toptvkorea](https://www.toptvkorea.com/news/524316). An aggregator snippet lists 2025 revenue of 127억, likely standalone **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Full capital impairment: consolidated equity of about -140억.** Borrowing shifted from banks (신한, IBK) to a non-bank lender at 13–14% interest, backed by receivables and personal guarantees. Current liabilities exceed current assets by about 198억, and **the auditor flagged going-concern uncertainty** — [이투데이](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2589725) (search snippet; the article returned 404 on fetch)
- Pre-IPO (May 2026): targeting 150억, with 100억 secured as mezzanine at about 1,000억 valuation. It is seeking strategic investors, with put-option downside protection if the IPO fails. Underwriter is NH투자증권. Cumulative investment exceeds 1,000억. A 280억 bridge round was raised earlier — [Daum 2026-05-11](https://v.daum.net/v/20260511090115291); [Nate 2026-05-18](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260518n24084); [Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/208764); [더벨 2025-02](https://www.thebell.co.kr/front/newsview.asp?key=202502061718173600108754)
- Headcount was 58 as of Aug 2025 (aggregator) — [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/fitpet) (search snippet)

**바잇미 (Biteme): small, profitable, brand-centric D2C platform**
- 2025: GMV 220억, operating profit 7억, Japan revenue about +300% YoY — [한국경제TV 2026-03-17](https://www.wowtv.co.kr/NewsCenter/News/Read?articleId=A202603170260); [Nate](https://news.nate.com/view/20260317n06221)
- Series A of 45억. Cumulative funding 93억 as of 2022 — [넥스트유니콘](https://www.nextunicorn.kr/content/b096d2c7a3b16bc6); [Innoforest](https://www.innoforest.co.kr/company/CP00002173)

**포동 (LG U+): telco pet "super-app" attempt**
- Launched July 2022 and open to non-LG U+ users. It passed 300,000 sign-ups in Sept 2023, 14 months after launch. Features: DBTI dog-personality test, a "고민 상담소" for owner questions, a community, a magazine with **pet-facility info**, and 포동스쿨 training classes — [LG U+ newsroom](https://news.lguplus.com/13591); [전자신문 2023-09](https://www.etnews.com/20230914000027)
- LG U+ bought its third pet-care company in 2023, aiming at a "700만 집사 플랫폼" — [한국경제 2023-11](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2023111204441)

**펫띵 (Pething, 더식스데이): subscription brand**
- Described as Korea's first pick-your-own pet-supplies subscription, rebranded from 돌로박스. It reported more than 10,000 customers in 3 years and a 100억 investment from 네이버 (2021 reporting). It also sells through SSG/이마트몰 — [파이낸셜뉴스 2021-07](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202107251757011211); [노트펫](https://www.notepet.co.kr/news/article/article_view/?idx=21627); [SSG brand shop](http://www.ssg.com/disp/brandShop.ssg?brandId=3000049049&ctgId=6000174588). **[UNVERIFIED: 2025–26 activity and financials.]**

**Horizontal marketplaces (쿠팡, 네이버, SSG, 11번가)**
- Share of total Korean e-commerce GMV: 쿠팡 22.7%, 네이버 20.7% — [세계비즈 2025-10](https://m.segyebiz.com/newsView/20251009506580) (search snippet). This is overall share, not the pet category.
- Online channels account for about 70% of pet-supplies sales (2024), up about 20%p since 2019 — search snippet attributed to [세계비즈](https://m.segyebiz.com/newsView/20251009506580) / [retailtalk](https://retailtalk.co.kr/Trend/?bmode=view&idx=98132372). **[source attribution not confirmed]**
- The Korean pet-food market is about 2조원, and **only about 7% is distributed through animal hospitals** — [데일리벳](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/industry/215934) (headline)
- Pet insurance comparison is now inside the fintech super-apps. **카카오페이** launched pet-insurance compare/recommend in July 2024 (삼성화재, 현대해상, KB손보, later DB손보). **네이버페이** launched in Nov 2024 (메리츠, DB, KB, 현대해상) — [한국경제 2024-10](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2024102193821); [블로터](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=624887)

**Adjacent apps (not commerce-core, but they compete for the same "all-in-one" attention)**
- 포인핸드: adoption plus lost-animal search, #2 pet app by MAU in H1 2023 (168,805) — [Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/210575); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=lost.animal.main&hl=en_US)
- 반려생활: pet-travel booking with a pet-friendly place search that includes 동물병원 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/%EB%B0%98%EB%A0%A4%EC%83%9D%ED%99%9C-%EB%B0%98%EB%A0%A4%EB%8F%99%EB%AC%BC-1%EB%93%B1-%EC%97%AC%ED%96%89%EC%95%B1/id1441374822)
- 비마이펫: knowledge platform with more than 20,000 pet-info articles — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1506523332)

### Inferences
- The commerce layer is effectively won by 펫프렌즈 plus 쿠팡/네이버. Standalone pet commerce margins are razor-thin even for the leader (0.4% operating margin in 2025). Challenging on commerce would be unattractive, which supports 펫안심365's non-commerce model.
- **GS리테일 is exiting or reducing exposure across its pet assets.** It is selling 어바웃펫 and is a passive 29.9% holder in 펫프렌즈, which IMM is trying to sell. Corporate "pet super-app" bets (GS, LG U+ 포동) have not produced durable standalone businesses. This suggests the super-app thesis is weak in Korea unless it is anchored in high-frequency commerce.
- 핏펫, the closest "health + commerce + hospital + insurance" integrated player, is financially fragile: capital impaired, going-concern flag, high-interest borrowing. Its B2C health-record features may be deprioritized. It may also become an acquirer's asset or a potential partner.
- 펫프렌즈 explicitly plans to move into healthcare and insurance. It is the most likely incumbent to add "care" features into a large existing user base. A defensible position for 펫안심365 would rest on official public data, emergency guidance and reporting workflows, which commerce apps are unlikely to prioritize.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Mobile Index or WiseApp MAU ranking for pet apps was found (latest is H1 2023).
- DART filings were not opened directly. 어바웃펫's full-year 2025 results and the sale's closing or buyer are not confirmed.
- 펫생각 and 올웨이즈펫: no evidence found that these exist as Korean pet platforms. The names may be mistaken, or refer to minor sellers.
- SSG/이마트 and 11번가 pet-category strategy, and 쿠팡/네이버 pet-category GMV, were not researched or found.
- 포동's 2025–26 status is unknown.

Commerce-core platforms concentrate on shopping, content and community. "Care" features appear as add-ons: 핏펫's test-result records and hospital operations, 어바웃펫's paid vet consultation and insurance membership, and 펫프렌즈's stated future plans. ### Inferences
- **Gaps among commerce and super-apps:**
  - No emergency or poison-exposure triage guidance.
  - No map built on official public data (licensed animal hospitals and 동물등록 대행기관 from 농림축산식품부 / 지자체 data).

  - No zoonosis info.
  - No neutral insurance-terms checker. Insurance comparison is controlled by fintechs and insurers with commercial incentives.

## Q3. Revenue models

### Takeaway
All the commerce-core players earn from product margin, with advertising and membership as secondary streams. The integrated players (핏펫) add diagnostics, supplements, hospital operations and non-pet businesses (gifticons) to support revenue. None monetizes mainly through verified local-business profiles.

### Cited Findings
- 펫프렌즈: product sales, plus a "high-margin advertising service" (brand ads inside the platform), plus in-house logistics efficiency — [Venturesquare](https://www.venturesquare.net/1046147)
- 어바웃펫: product sales, plus a paid membership (실비클럽, 19,900원/month, c.2024) — [모비인사이드](https://www.mobiinside.co.kr/2024/03/21/petfriends-2/) (search snippet)
- 핏펫: diagnostic kits, supplements, commerce, distribution and logistics, offline animal hospitals, plus the gifticon marketplace (니콘내콘, about 440억 of 2024 revenue) — [머니투데이](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2025/05/04/2025050211330580002); [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260511090115291)
- 바잇미: brand-centric D2C commerce with overseas (Japan) expansion — [한국경제TV](https://www.wowtv.co.kr/NewsCenter/News/Read?articleId=A202603170260)
- Fintech super-apps: insurance distribution fees through comparison services — [블로터](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=624887)

### Inferences
- Ad-funded commerce creates an incentive for **sponsored placement over neutral information**. A trust-first safety app can differentiate by clearly separating verified partner profiles from editorial safety guidance.
- Local pet businesses (clinics, groomers, hotels) are not the paying customer for any of these commerce platforms. That B2B niche looks under-served by the commerce leaders (inference; not directly sourced).

### Gaps
- No disclosed ad-revenue share for 펫프렌즈 or others.
- Commission and take rates were not found.

## Q4. Pain points: trust, ads, information quality

### Takeaway
Evidence of consumer distrust is strongest at the **product-quality and labeling** level: about 40% of surveyed owners report dissatisfaction or harm, and 한국소비자원 records thousands of quality complaints. App-review-level complaints about specific platforms were not collected in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Survey of 1,000 pet owners: about **40% reported dissatisfaction or harm** related to product quality, safety or label mismatch. 62.1% buy expensive foreign products, citing safety (72.2%) and durability (59.7%). 30.2% want a quality certification system as the top regulatory fix — [서울신문 2025-10-23](https://www.seoul.co.kr/news/politics/2025/10/23/20251023500231)
- 한국소비자원: of 14,891 pet-related consumer consultations over the past 5 years, 5,006 (about 37%) concerned quality, the largest category. In a safety test of 30 overseas-direct-purchase pet products, 20 (66.7%) contained harmful substances above standards — [서울신문](https://www.seoul.co.kr/news/politics/2025/10/23/20251023500231); [한국소비자원 해외직구 반려동물 용품 안전실태조사](https://www.kca.go.kr/smartconsumer/board/download.do?fno=10043910&bid=00000146&did=1003733454&menukey=7301)
- 한국소비자원 found pet-food labels hard to understand. It recommended plainer ingredient names and voluntary label/ad improvement by industry — [푸드아이콘](https://www.foodicon.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=15235); [한국소비자원](https://www.kca.go.kr/home/sub.do?menukey=4002&mode=view&no=1003856891)
- The KB 2025 한국 반려동물 보고서 (1,000-owner survey plus FGD) focuses special issues on pet obesity and pet loss — [KB금융지주 경영연구소](https://www.kbfg.com/kbresearch/report/reportView.do?reportId=2000531) (report not read in detail)
- Industry commentary describes pet commerce as a "만년 적자" sector amid sale issues ("혼돈의 카오스"). This signals platform instability, which matters for users who store data such as pet profiles and health records — [모비인사이드 2024-03](https://www.mobiinside.co.kr/2024/03/21/petfriends-2/)

### Inferences
- There is strong demand for **trustworthy, official, verified information** (certification, safety standards, recall and harmful-substance data). This fits 펫안심365's public-data and safety positioning, for example surfacing 한국소비자원 recall and safety results.
- Platform instability (어바웃펫's sale, 핏펫's going-concern flag) is a trust angle. Users' pet health data held by distressed commerce apps may be at risk of discontinuation. A neutral, exportable safety record could be a selling point (inference).

### Gaps
- No systematic Google Play or App Store review mining for 펫프렌즈, 어바웃펫 or 핏펫 was done. Complaints specifically about ads, spam push notifications, or fake reviews inside these apps are not evidenced here.
- No survey found specifically on trust in pet apps or online vet information quality in 2025–26.
