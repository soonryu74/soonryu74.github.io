# Korean Pet Healthcare, Vet-Access and Pet-Insurance Platforms (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-09-25. Scope: Korea only. Figures are labeled with year and source. Items marked **[unverified]** could not be confirmed from a primary or reliable secondary source within this research pass.

## 1. Clinic finder / booking, health-record, AI and telehealth apps, and clinic EMR vendors

### Takeaway
The consumer vet-access layer is fragmented and mostly owned by vertically integrated players: EMR vendors (인투씨엔에스/인투펫, 우리엔) use chart integration to push booking and health records; commerce players (핏펫) bundle at-home test kits, booking and a health notebook; AI startups (에이아이포펫/티티케어) are the only players legally running vet telehealth, and only inside a regulatory sandbox. The first-generation vet-consult app 펫닥 has suspended its app. No player found positions itself as a neutral "safety" layer (emergency guidance, public-data map, insurance-terms explanation).

### Inferences
- EMR vendors hold the structured clinic data (visits, vaccines, receipts). An independent safety app cannot easily get record sync without partnering with 인투씨엔에스 or 우리엔. The practical starting points are owner-entered records and receipt capture.
- 펫닥's suspension suggests that consumer vet Q&A and consult apps have struggled to monetize. Treat this as an inference; no financials were found.
- Public-data clinic maps built on LOCALDATA already exist (NearVet). Differentiation has to come from "actually open now or verified 24h" status, which is a known data gap, plus emergency triage content, rather than from the map itself.
- Telehealth remains sandbox-only, limited to follow-up care after an in-person visit. A "guidance without diagnosis" stance for 펫안심365 fits current law.

### Gaps
- 똑닥 pet service, 펫에게, 우리동물병원 app and 벳플러스: no evidence found that these exist as pet clinic-booking products. **[unverified]**
- MAU, download counts and revenue for 인투펫, 핏펫, 티티케어 and 펫트라슈 were not found.
- Current (2025–26) EMR market shares were not found; the share claims above are older.
- No confirmed number of real 24h clinics, and no official emergency-clinic registry (none appears to exist).

## 2. Pet insurance market: insurers, share, penetration, premiums, government measures, comparison platforms

### Takeaway
Korean pet insurance is small but growing fast: about 250k in-force policies and ₩128.7bn in premiums in 2025, and about 307k policies in H1 2026. Penetration is still in the 1% range. 메리츠화재 (펫퍼민트) leads with roughly half the market. Policy since 2023 has pushed three things: specialist insurers (마이브라운 launched in July 2025), easier claims, and platform comparison (카카오페이 from 2024, then 네이버페이). The May 2025 FSS product reform (1-year renewal, 30% copay, ₩30k minimum deductible) made products less generous, and comparison-platform uptake was weak.

**Claims pain points**
- The same condition gets different diagnosis names at different clinics (e.g., "위장염" vs "복통·구토·설사"). Receipts are not standardized, and some bundle medical and non-medical items. Claims need repeated rounds of document requests, and insurers ask for more material when details are unclear. — [파이낸셜뉴스 2026-09-07](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609071818114472); [네이트/뉴스 2025-12-17](https://news.nate.com/view/20251217n19708)
- "Automatic" or real-time claims are emerging (마이브라운 라이브청구, "현장 지급"). Example: on a ₩1m surgery, ₩700k of insurance is deducted at checkout. — [보험신보](https://www.insweek.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=72399); [네이트 2026-03-17](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260317n27758)

### Inferences
- The May 2025 reform made products more complex for owners to understand: 1-year renewal, deductible plus copay, exclusions, and re-underwriting at renewal. That raises demand for a neutral terms explainer ("what will this policy actually pay for X?").
- Comparison platforms are licensed intermediaries that earn fees and compare on price. A non-selling tool that explains terms, deductibles and exclusions, and checks whether a condition or claim is covered, fills a different niche and stays clear of intermediary licensing. The legal boundary with 보험업법 "모집/중개" should still be confirmed by counsel.
- Real-time claims tie insurers to partner clinics (마이브라운 with 우리엔 as investor-EMR). Claims convenience will likely stay insurer- and EMR-controlled.

## 3. Vet fee transparency rules and telehealth legality

### Takeaway
Korea now requires clinics to post fees for a set list of common items, applied to all clinics since 2024-01-05, and to explain and give estimated costs before major treatment (since 2023-01-05). Diagnosis and procedure names were standardized in 2025, but only as a non-binding recommendation, and there is still no binding fee schedule. Telehealth is illegal under 수의사법 §12 (direct examination rule) except inside a limited sandbox.

### Cited Findings
- The amended 수의사법 requires, before major treatment such as surgery or transfusion, an explanation of diagnosis, necessity, side effects and owner duties, written consent, and notice of the estimated cost. ## 4. Remaining gaps for an independent safety and insurance-understanding tool (for 펫안심365)

### Takeaway
Based on the above, no found player combines neutral emergency guidance, a verified open-now or 24h clinic map, insurance-terms explanation without selling, and fee and receipt literacy. Incumbents are either sellers (insurers, comparison platforms that earn 3% fees), clinic-data owners (EMR vendors), or commerce (핏펫).

### Cited Findings
- There is no official standard for 24h or emergency clinics, and "24시" signage is unreliable at night. — [thinkingcatworks](https://blog.thinkingcatworks.com/posts/introducing-nearvet-nearby-animal-hospital-finder)
- Comparison platforms are fee-based (about 3%) and had weak product listing after the 2025 reform. — [뉴스토마토](https://www.newstomato.com/readnews.aspx?no=1262496); [FINS](http://www.fins.co.kr/news/articleViewAmp.html?idxno=99736)
- Since May 2025, products carry 1-year renewal, a 30% copay and a ₩30k minimum deductible, adding complexity. — [데일리벳](https://www.dailyvet.co.kr/news/industry/242198)
- Claims friction comes from non-standard diagnosis names and receipts. — [파이낸셜뉴스 2026-09-07](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609071818114472)
- The owner's right to get medical records was reportedly strengthened in Sept 2026. — [파이낸셜뉴스](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609071818114472)

### Inferences
- **Terms checker** (no recommendation): explain renewal, copay, deductibles, exclusions and pre-existing conditions across products, and let users check whether a scenario would be covered and why. Base it on public 약관 and 상품요약서. It must avoid ranking or recommending, to stay outside 보험 모집/중개 rules; legal review is needed.
- **Receipt and records helper**: guide owners to request medical records under the new right and map clinic line items to the 2025 standard codes. This supports faster claims without being a claim agent.
- **Emergency layer**: build a symptom-to-urgency guide that does not diagnose, which is consistent with 수의사법 §12. Pair it with crowd- or partner-verified "open now or truly 24h" status. That B2B partner-verification could be the monetizable asset, since public LOCALDATA lacks night-operation truth.
- **B2B partner profiles**: clinics already must post fees. Verified partner profiles could show posted fees in a standard format, turning compliance into marketing. EMR vendors (인투씨엔에스, 우리엔) are potential partners or competitors.
- **Risks**: 메리츠, 마이브라운 and the platforms may add their own explainers. Telehealth expansion could change the triage landscape. The 대한수의사회 has historically resisted anything that looks like diagnosis or records disclosure.

### Gaps
- No user-research data was found on owners' understanding of pet-insurance terms or on complaint volumes; the 한국소비자원 report in section 2 is a likely source.
- No data was found on how many clinics post fees online, or on compliance rates.
