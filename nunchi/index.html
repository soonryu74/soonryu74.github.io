A real situation you will actually be in. Nothing gets corrected while you talk — mistakes are collected and shown afterwards, so you can keep going.

Tip: don't worry about mistakes. That's what the review cards are for.

Two kinds of feedback. WRONG is a grammar error. ODD is correct grammar that a Korean would not say — the part no other app tells you.
