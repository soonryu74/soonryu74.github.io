Story

A painting is made in solitude,
yet it cannot reach the world alone.

IThirty Years

Thirty Years Before the Canvas

For thirty years, I never set down the brush. Nights spent waiting for paint to dry; a hand that lingered long between the finished and the unfinished.

Those years taught me one thing: behind every painting lies an entire human life. And so, before the work, I look to the one who made it.

IISix Years

Six Years Before the Audience

Standing in galleries as a docent, I came to see the other side of the canvas — hurried footsteps stopped by a single sentence, and in that pause, eyes beginning to change.

There I learned that a work is loved not when it is explained, but when it is understood.

IIIIn Between

Standing In Between

The silence of those who create, the hesitation of those who look. To build a bridge between them — this is the place I found after a long walk.

Instead of grand capital, I bring thirty years of seeing and six years of speaking, and I stand beside the artist — so that the artist may stand before nothing but the canvas.

IVOne Breath

Carrying One Person's Breath

On Oh Jiyoon's canvases, thousands of layered brushstrokes breathe like waves. The light held in gold and pearl powder is a light only endurance can make.

As that breath travels to Venice and into the heart of Seoul, I walk that road beside her.

If there is a painting the world has not yet seen,
still waiting for its first audience —
let me be the first to hear its story.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Can emerging, unknown artists reach out?

Of course. Recognising work the world has not yet seen is exactly what I do. Send a few images of your work and simply say hello.

What does artist management cost?

I ask for no upfront fees. As a rule, we share in the reward when sales, patronage or exhibitions bear fruit. All terms are discussed transparently in our first conversation.

Must we purchase works for our corporate space?

Both purchase and loan are possible. I will propose the best approach for your space and budget.

Who are the docent talks for?

From first-time visitors to collectors' circles and corporate teams — each talk is tailored, accessible, and made to stay with you.
